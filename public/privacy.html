SecondBrain Privacy Notice

Pre-launch pilot draft. The operator identity, privacy contact details, grievance contact, and final legal review must be completed before public launch.

Last updated: 13 October 2026.

What we process

SecondBrain may process account identifiers, preferences, conversations, tasks, notes, reminders, calendar data, memories, documents and extracted document content, usage telemetry, AI run metadata, connected-service metadata, billing status, and organization/workspace data that you provide or generate while using the service.

Connected services

If you connect Google or GitHub, SecondBrain stores encrypted authorization credentials needed to access the permissions you grant. Google authorization is revoked where possible when you disconnect it. Local connection credentials are removed when the connection is disconnected. GitHub provider-side revocation remains a manual launch requirement.

AI processing

Prompts and relevant context may be sent to configured AI providers to generate responses. The application uses usage controls, context minimization, caching, and provider fallback logic. Sensitive actions remain subject to approval controls where implemented.

Your controls

You can review or remove saved memories, disconnect integrations, and download a JSON export of your application data from Settings. Account-closure requests are handled through an authenticated, admin-reviewed workflow during the pilot because the underlying end-user identity is managed by the hosting platform.

Retention

Product data is retained while needed to provide the service unless you request erasure or a shorter period is configured. Transient OAuth state, rate-limit counters, AI telemetry, tool telemetry, operational logs, payment records, and audit records may have different retention periods. Financial and audit records may need to be retained where required by law or legitimate compliance needs.

Security

SecondBrain uses authenticated user routes, per-user data scoping, encrypted OAuth credentials, server-only secrets, rate limits, operational monitoring, and access controls. No system can guarantee absolute security.

India data-protection readiness

The service is being prepared for the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 on their phased commencement timeline. Final compliance should be reviewed by qualified counsel before public launch.
